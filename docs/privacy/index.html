Privacy

This website

krouton.app and archive.krouton.app are plain pages served by GitHub Pages. They set no cookies, run no analytics, and load no fonts, scripts or images from anyone else. GitHub, as the host, receives the usual request details, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement.

Krouton and Krouton Server

Neither has an account, sends usage data, or checks in with us. Krouton Server talks only to the Macs you pair with it, and by default only on your own network. Disc images and folders you burn stay on your computers.

Drive reports

Reports you send are published in the open drive record. Leave your name out if you’d rather not have it published.

Krouton is built in the open at github.com/jigaz/Krouton. Contact: jorgen@krouton.app.
